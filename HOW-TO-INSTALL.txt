FX CONVERTER: PUT IT ONLINE AND INSTALL ON ANDROID
==================================================

A. Put it online (free, about 10 minutes, one time only)
   1. Unzip fx-converter.zip on your computer.
   2. Go to github.com and sign up (free) or sign in.
   3. Click "+" (top right) > "New repository". Name it fx-converter,
      choose Public, tick "Add a README file", click "Create repository".
   4. Click "Add file" > "Upload files". Drag in EVERYTHING from the
      unzipped folder: index.html, manifest.json, sw.js and the icons
      folder. Click "Commit changes".
   5. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" choose "main" and "/ (root)",
      then click Save.
   6. Wait 1-2 minutes and refresh that page. It shows your app address,
      like https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/fx-converter/

B. Install on your Android phone
   1. Open that address in Chrome on the phone.
   2. Tap the menu (three dots) > "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
   3. The FX icon appears with your other apps. It opens full screen.

Notes
   - Rates are fetched live each time you open the app (the source updates
     once a day). The status shows LIVE, SAVED RATES or OFFLINE.
   - Works offline using the last rates it downloaded.
   - The address is public, but the app only shows exchange rates and
     stores nothing about you except your last currency pair on the phone.
   - To share it with staff, send them the address; they install the same way.
   - Rates source: Exchange Rate API (open.er-api.com), mid-market rates.
     Banks add a margin, so use "Use my bank's rates" for exact quotes.
